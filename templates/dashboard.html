Deployment Summary

This Flask application deploys the optimized Random Forest machine learning pipeline developed during Phase 1. The platform accepts physicochemical properties as input, applies the preprocessing operations included in the trained pipeline, predicts the wine quality score, and presents SHAP-based explanations together with analytical visualisations generated during model development.
